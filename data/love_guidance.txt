# Love & Relationship Guidance Knowledge Base
# astro-agent | RAG Knowledge Corpus
# Format: [CATEGORY] followed by guidance lines
# Categories: GENERAL, per zodiac sign, planetary influence, elemental

[GENERAL]
- Emotional clarity strengthens all relationships — know what you feel before expressing it.
- Try communicating your feelings openly rather than expecting others to read your mind.
- Avoid arguments driven by ego or impatience — they rarely resolve what actually needs healing.
- Show appreciation for the small gestures; love is built in the ordinary moments.
- Relationships require consistent effort, not just grand romantic acts.
- Boundaries are not walls — they are the foundation of healthy, lasting love.
- Periods of distance in a relationship are often invitations to grow individually.
- Trust is built slowly and broken quickly — protect it with honesty and consistency.
- Love is not just a feeling; it is a daily choice to show up for another person.
- Forgiveness does not mean forgetting — it means releasing the hold a wound has over you.

[ARIES]
- Your passion and directness are magnetic — use them to initiate, not to dominate.
- Independence is vital to you; seek a partner who respects your need for autonomy.
- Avoid letting impatience push you to move faster than a relationship is ready for.
- Your love is fierce and loyal once committed — let potential partners see that depth.
- This period favors bold romantic gestures and honest conversations about where things stand.
- Conflict in your relationship right now can be resolved through direct, calm dialogue.
- Avoid competitive dynamics in love — your partner is not a rival.
- Physical affection and shared adventures keep your romantic energy alive.

[TAURUS]
- Your loyalty and steadiness make you one of the most devoted partners of the zodiac.
- You need emotional and financial security to fully open your heart — that is valid.
- Avoid possessiveness masking as love — trust must be given as well as earned.
- This period favors deepening commitment, having honest conversations about the future.
- Small, sensory acts of love — cooking together, physical touch, shared comfort — recharge your bond.
- If something feels off in your relationship, address it before stubbornness turns it into a wall.
- A long-term partnership may reach a meaningful milestone or conversation this month.
- Your love is not showy but it is real — make sure your partner knows how deeply you care.

[GEMINI]
- Intellectual connection is your deepest form of intimacy — you need a partner who engages your mind.
- Avoid emotional inconsistency confusing those who love you — be clear about your feelings.
- This period favors open, playful conversations that bring back lightness to your relationship.
- Boredom is your relationship's greatest enemy — introduce novelty, ideas, and new experiences.
- Your wit and charm attract many — focus that energy on the person who truly matters.
- A relationship may deepen significantly when you move past surface-level conversation.
- Listen as much as you speak — deep listening is an act of love Geminis must consciously practice.
- Short trips or shared learning experiences with your partner strengthen your bond now.

[CANCER]
- You love with extraordinary depth and devotion — protect that gift by choosing people worthy of it.
- Your intuition about a partner's emotional state is rarely wrong — trust it.
- Avoid withdrawing into your shell when hurt — vulnerability is the path to deeper connection.
- This period favors creating safe, nurturing spaces for emotional honesty in your relationship.
- Home and shared domestic rituals are love languages for you — lean into them.
- If you have been carrying emotional weight alone, now is the time to let your partner in.
- Old emotional wounds may surface — use them as opportunities to heal, not to close off.
- A new romantic connection forming now has the potential for deep, lasting roots.

[LEO]
- You love generously and dramatically — your partner should feel like the most adored person alive.
- Avoid needing constant validation in love — your worth is not determined by admiration.
- This period favors romantic grand gestures, quality time, and celebrating your relationship openly.
- Your loyalty, once given, is unshakeable — make sure your partner truly deserves it.
- Avoid letting pride prevent you from apologizing when you are wrong — humility deepens love.
- A partner who matches your warmth and appreciates your generosity is your ideal companion.
- Playfulness and creativity in your relationship keep the flame strong — plan something memorable.
- If a relationship has felt flat, reignite it by bringing your full, radiant self back into it.

[VIRGO]
- You show love through service, care, and attention to detail — make sure that is also received as love.
- Avoid over-analyzing your relationship to the point of manufacturing problems that do not exist.
- This period favors practical acts of love — solving a problem together, supporting a partner's goals.
- Your standards are high, but be careful they do not become a barrier to genuine connection.
- Expressing vulnerability is uncomfortable for you but transformative for your relationships.
- A partner who appreciates your devotion and does not exploit your giving nature is your match.
- Health and wellness routines shared with a partner can become a meaningful form of intimacy.
- If criticism has crept into your relationship, replace it with specific appreciation this month.

[LIBRA]
- You are the zodiac's most romantic sign — love is not just desired by you, it is essential.
- Avoid losing yourself in a relationship trying to keep the peace — your needs matter too.
- This period favors honest conversations about what you need from your relationship.
- Your charm and grace make you deeply attractive — own that without guilt.
- Indecision in matters of the heart is your greatest challenge — trust your feelings over logic.
- A relationship built on mutual respect and aesthetic harmony is your deepest aspiration.
- If you have been avoiding a difficult romantic conversation, the time to have it is now.
- Shared beauty experiences — art, music, travel — are acts of love that fill your soul.

[SCORPIO]
- You love with volcanic intensity — all or nothing, and nothing in between.
- Avoid letting jealousy or control erode the trust that took so long to build.
- This period favors deep, transformative conversations that take your relationship to a new level.
- Your loyalty is absolute — ensure it is reciprocated before offering it completely.
- Emotional walls you have built for protection may now be safe to lower with the right person.
- Intimacy for you goes far beyond the physical — you seek psychological and spiritual merger.
- If betrayal has occurred, you must decide: forgive and rebuild, or release with dignity.
- A karmic relationship may be completing its cycle — honor what it taught you.

[SAGITTARIUS]
- Your love is adventurous, generous, and freedom-loving — you need a partner who can keep up.
- Avoid commitmentphobia masking as freedom-seeking — some roots allow deeper flight.
- This period favors exploring new experiences together that reignite excitement in your bond.
- Honesty is your strongest love value — but delivery with kindness makes the truth land better.
- A partner who shares your philosophical curiosity and loves to explore will hold your heart long.
- Long-distance or cross-cultural romantic connections carry positive energy this period.
- Avoid taking a stable, loving relationship for granted while chasing the next horizon.
- Laughter and shared adventure are your most powerful love languages — use them freely.

[CAPRICORN]
- You approach love seriously and with great intentionality — that is a gift, not a flaw.
- Avoid letting career ambition crowd out the time and presence your relationship needs.
- This period favors making concrete commitments, having practical conversations about the future.
- Emotional warmth may not come naturally, but your acts of provision and loyalty speak loudly.
- Allow yourself to be vulnerable with your partner — strength includes the courage to be seen.
- A relationship that has been tested by time and hardship is proving its worth right now.
- Your partner needs to know they are a priority, not a footnote to your ambitions.
- Quality, intentional time together matters more to your bond than quantity of time.

[AQUARIUS]
- You love unconventionally and fiercely on your own terms — that is your right.
- Avoid emotional detachment reading as indifference — your partner needs warmth, not just intellect.
- This period favors honest, non-judgmental conversations about what your relationship truly means.
- Friendship is the foundation of all your deepest loves — nurture that friendship always.
- You need a partner who respects your independence and shares your vision for a better world.
- Romantic connections formed through shared causes or intellectual pursuits are deeply aligned for you.
- Avoid rebellion for its own sake in love — sometimes stability is a radical and beautiful choice.
- Emotional intimacy grows when you let your partner see the feeling person behind the ideas.

[PISCES]
- You love with a depth and selflessness that few signs can match — protect that sacred gift.
- Avoid losing yourself completely in another person — you need your own shore to return to.
- This period favors romantic depth, spiritual connection, and creative expressions of love.
- Your intuition about a partner's unspoken feelings is extraordinarily accurate — trust it.
- Beware of idealizing partners and missing red flags — see people clearly as well as lovingly.
- Love for you is a spiritual experience — seek partners who honor that depth.
- Creative expressions of love — art, music, poetry, cooking — speak your heart better than words.
- If a relationship has been draining you, it may be time to honestly evaluate what it is giving back.

[PLANET_SUN]
- Sun-activated love phases bring clarity about what you truly want from a relationship.
- Your confidence and magnetism are heightened — you attract partners through authentic self-expression.
- This is a period for honest conversations about identity and role within a partnership.
- Leo-Sun energy in relationships: you radiate warmth but need to resist the urge to dominate.
- A Sun return or solar transit to your 7th house often marks a significant relationship revelation.
- Avoid choosing partners based solely on how much they admire you — seek genuine mutual respect.

[PLANET_MERCURY]
- Mercury-influenced relationship phases are driven by communication, wit, and intellectual connection.
- Difficult conversations that have been avoided are best addressed during Mercury direct phases.
- Misunderstandings and miscommunications are common during Mercury retrograde — choose words with extra care.
- Shared learning, travel, and lively debate are love languages for Mercury-activated periods.
- Writing to a partner — letters, notes, heartfelt messages — carries exceptional impact now.
- A connection formed through intellectual exchange or shared curiosity carries strong Mercury relationship energy.

[PLANET_VENUS]
- Venus transits bring heightened romantic energy, attractiveness, and opportunities for new love.
- Existing relationships deepen in warmth and affection under Venus influence.
- Financial and romantic abundance often arrive together during Venus-activated phases.
- Aesthetic experiences shared with a partner — art, music, beauty — are especially meaningful now.
- A Venus return to your natal position marks a powerful period for love and self-worth.

[PLANET_MOON]
- Moon-influenced relationship phases are emotionally intense and deeply revealing.
- Intuition about a partner's needs is sharper — respond with empathy, not analysis.
- Full Moon periods bring relationship tensions to the surface for resolution.
- New Moon periods are ideal for setting intentions about the kind of love you want to invite.
- Emotional security and domestic harmony are love priorities during Moon-activated phases.

[PLANET_MARS]
- Mars brings passion, physical attraction, and desire intensity to relationships.
- Sexual and romantic chemistry is heightened — existing relationships feel renewed energy.
- Avoid Mars-fueled arguments becoming destructive — passion and anger share the same fire.
- New attractions forming under Mars influence are intense but require patience to build lasting roots.
- Courage to pursue someone you have been hesitant about is supported by Mars energy.

[PLANET_JUPITER]
- Jupiter expands love — relationships grow deeper, commitments become more serious.
- New romantic connections forming under Jupiter carry exceptional long-term potential.
- Generosity and gratitude in your relationship attract more of what you are appreciating.
- Marriage, engagements, and major relationship milestones are supported by Jupiter transits.
- Love that has a philosophical or spiritual dimension flourishes under Jupiter's influence.

[PLANET_SATURN]
- Saturn tests relationships to reveal their true foundations.
- Relationships that survive Saturn transits emerge stronger and more committed.
- Superficial or karmic connections that no longer serve growth often end during Saturn phases.
- This is a period for seriousness, honesty, and evaluating what you truly want from love.
- Saturn rewards patient, respectful, and consistent love — shortcuts and games backfire now.

[PLANET_RAHU]
- Rahu brings intense, karmic, and sometimes obsessive attractions into your life.
- A relationship forming under Rahu may feel destined — proceed with awareness, not just feeling.
- Unconventional or cross-cultural romantic connections carry strong Rahu energy.
- Avoid illusions in love during Rahu phases — see your partner clearly, not just as you wish them to be.

[PLANET_KETU]
- Ketu brings karmic completion to relationships — what was unfinished in past cycles surfaces now.
- Feelings of spiritual connection or inexplicable familiarity with someone signal Ketu influence.
- Some relationships may end during Ketu phases — release with gratitude, not bitterness.
- Solitude and inner reflection during Ketu phases are acts of self-love that prepare you for deeper connection.

[ELEMENT_FIRE]
- Fire signs (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius) love with passion, enthusiasm, and fierce loyalty.
- They need excitement and growth in relationships — stagnation is love's enemy for fire signs.
- Fire sign love thrives on admiration, adventure, and a partner who matches their energy.

[ELEMENT_EARTH]
- Earth signs (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn) build love slowly, steadily, and with lasting intention.
- Physical presence, acts of service, and material security are their primary love languages.
- Earth sign love is not dramatic but it is deeply reliable — and that is its greatest beauty.

[ELEMENT_AIR]
- Air signs (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius) fall in love with minds first and hearts second.
- Communication, intellectual exchange, and shared ideas are the foundation of air sign love.
- Freedom within partnership is non-negotiable — give air signs space and they will always return.

[ELEMENT_WATER]
- Water signs (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces) love with extraordinary emotional depth and sensitivity.
- They need emotional safety and genuine intimacy — surface-level connections do not satisfy.
- Water sign love is healing, intuitive, and transformative when it finds its true match.
